Food Plan

Vegetarian, for 3 people, split roughly 50/50 between DIY at camp and eating out.

Orick and Klamath are food deserts — one traveler even reported no vegetarian food nearby in Klamath, requiring a drive to Crescent City. Shop before you arrive (Bay Area, Arcata, or Eureka), and treat eating out as a planned stop rather than something you'll find on the fly.

DIY at Camp

Breakfast
Oatmeal with nuts, dried fruit & cinnamon — or breakfast burritos with scrambled egg/tofu, black beans, cheese & salsa.
Lunch
Pita or bread with hummus, avocado & pre-washed veggies — or a cold pasta salad (olives, feta, cherry tomatoes) made ahead.
Dinner
One-pot veggie chili, or foil-packet veggie skewers & corn over the fire, or pasta with jarred marinara & pre-chopped veggies.
Snacks
Trail mix, string cheese, apples, granola bars for grazing between hikes.

Eat Out

Good Harvest Café — Arcata area
Breakfast, lunch, dinner, explicit vegetarian fare, and sack lunches for the trail. Worth a stop on the drive up.
Historic Requa Inn — near Klamath
Good for breakfast, showcases local ingredients — more seafood-forward, so ask about vegetarian options.
Crescent City
Widest range of vegetarian-friendly restaurants in the area — best bet for a real sit-down dinner instead of cooking.
